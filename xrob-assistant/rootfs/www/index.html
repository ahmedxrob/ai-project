Telegram · setup and quick guide

  1. Create a bot with @BotFather and copy its token.
  2. Find your numeric chat ID using a chat-ID helper bot or Telegram getUpdates.
  3. In the add-on Configuration, fill telegram_bot_token and telegram_allowed_chat_id, save and restart.
  4. Open your bot and send /start.

How to use the app

  1. Planner: create an Event for appointments or a Task for something to do. Tasks can have a due date and time reminder.
  2. Fitness: log your weight, treadmill minutes, or gym session. Weight is limited to one record per day.
  3. Bills: add an amount and due date, then mark it paid when you pay it (the app never pays).
  4. Routines: set a repeat interval and target time. Late Telegram reminders are optional. When a late reminder is configured, you can enable Move to tomorrow if still incomplete; after the late Telegram alert is delivered, an unfinished routine moves to tomorrow. This option is hidden when no late reminder is set.
  5. Meals: save each meal as Home or Outside. Home meals track the ready plates on hand and pause when the stock is empty; outside meals do not use stock. XROB automatically schedules plates and shows them on the Planner calendar. Telegram sends today's plate at 14:00 and asks if you ate it at 22:00.
  6. Shopping list: keep a simple checklist of item names on the Meals page, tick items when bought, and add ingredients from planned meals.
  7. Overview: check what is coming up and what needs attention.

Data and privacy

All records are stored in /config/xrob_assistant.db inside the add-on persistent configuration mount. Back up the Home Assistant add-on configuration regularly.

No Google Calendar, cloud database or Ollama connection is used.
