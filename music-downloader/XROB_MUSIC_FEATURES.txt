============================================================
CURRENT 2.9.3 UPDATE
============================================================
- Spotify/Apple Music-inspired top navigation with Xrob logo identity.
- Search and Settings live in the top utility controls instead of the primary navigation row.
- Downloads opens as a compact floating activity drawer.
- Up Next queue opens from the persistent player and closes when clicking outside it or pressing Escape.
- Downloads drawer closes when clicking outside it or pressing Escape.
- Recently Added playback now shares the same persisted queue data used by Up Next while preserving its Home ordering.
- Home Tracks / Artists / Albums statistics open their corresponding Library views.
- Home metric icons use the centered presentation used by the earlier Xrob design.
- Browser tab favicon / app icon uses the Xrob icon asset.
- Play-count threshold is 60 seconds; short tracks retain the existing proportional short-track rule.
- Global player, queue controls, repeat, shuffle and persisted playback state remain enabled.

SUGGESTED PRIORITY
============================================================
P0 — build next: queue drag editing, playlists, favorites, lyrics, crossfade/gapless, Smart Mix, album/artist/playlist bulk download, duplicate detection, richer Recently Played/Most Played views, bulk metadata editor.

P1 — high value: smart playlists, mood/activity mixes, AI text-to-playlist, equalizer, ReplayGain, download priority, bandwidth controls, device/output selection, song credits, related artists, better analytics.

P2 — advanced: collaborative playlists, remote playback, spatial-audio metadata, editorial/liner-note content, recommendation feedback loops, smart downloads and richer cross-device features.

REFERENCES USED FOR THE ROADMAP
- Spotify official product/support updates: personalized DJ requests, Smart Shuffle, queue management and offline download improvements.
- Apple Music official pages/support: Shared Listening, collaborative playlists, AutoMix, Crossfade, lyrics translation/pronunciation and Sing.
- YouTube Music official help: Samples, Ask Music, Smart Downloads, offline playback, background play and audio-only mode.
- TIDAL official support: FLAC as the supported stereo lossless path and Dolby Atmos for immersive audio.
